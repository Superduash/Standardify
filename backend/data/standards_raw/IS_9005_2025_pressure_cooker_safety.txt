IS 9005:2025
PRESSURE COOKERS FOR DOMESTIC USE — SAFETY REQUIREMENTS AND TEST METHODS

1. SCOPE
1.1 This standard specifies the safety requirements and test methods for domestic pressure cookers
intended for cooking food on a gas stove, electric hot-plate, or induction hob. It applies to
pressure cookers with a working pressure not exceeding 100 kPa (1 bar) gauge and a capacity
from 1.5 L to 22 L.
1.2 Materials covered: stainless steel (grades 304 and 430), aluminum alloys, and anodised aluminum.
1.3 This standard does not apply to commercial catering pressure cookers or autoclaves.

2. REFERENCES
2.1 IS 2347:2017 — Specification for domestic pressure cookers (aluminium)
2.2 IS 9000:2025 [SAMPLE] — Safety Requirements for Household Electrical Appliances (referenced

2.3 IS 1570 (Part 5):1985 — Schedules for wrought steels: stainless and heat-resisting steels

3. DEFINITIONS
3.1 Working pressure: The maximum gauge pressure at which the pressure cooker is designed to
operate continuously, as set by the pressure-limiting device.
3.2 Safety valve: A valve that opens automatically to release excess pressure if the working
pressure is exceeded, preventing explosion.
3.3 Gasket: A ring of flexible material (typically silicone or rubber) that provides a pressure-
tight seal between the lid and the body of the cooker.

4. MATERIAL REQUIREMENTS
4.1 Body and lid: Shall be manufactured from stainless steel (minimum grade 304) or aluminium
alloy (minimum grade 3003). The wall thickness of the body and lid shall not be less than the
values specified in Table 1, based on the cooker capacity.
4.2 Gasket: Shall be manufactured from food-grade silicone or food-grade rubber conforming to
applicable BIS/ISO standards. Gaskets shall not contain any material that may impart taste,
odour, or toxic substances to food.
4.3 Handles: Shall be manufactured from heat-resistant material (bakelite, phenolic resin, or
equivalent) and shall be so designed that the cooker can be lifted safely when fully loaded.

5. SAFETY DEVICES
5.1 Primary pressure-limiting device: Each pressure cooker shall be fitted with a primary pressure-
limiting device (commonly a weight-type valve or spring-loaded valve) that opens at a pressure
not exceeding 115 kPa gauge and fully reseats when pressure falls below 80 kPa gauge.
5.2 Secondary safety device: Each pressure cooker shall additionally be fitted with a secondary
safety device that releases pressure if the primary device fails. This may be a fusible plug, a
rupture disc, or a secondary relief valve set at 140 kPa gauge maximum.
5.3 Interlock: The lid shall be equipped with a locking interlock mechanism that prevents opening
of the lid while the internal pressure exceeds 10 kPa gauge.
5.4 Overheat indicator: For electric or induction-compatible models, an audible or visual overheat
indicator shall actuate if the cooker body temperature exceeds 200°C.

6. PERFORMANCE AND TESTING REQUIREMENTS
6.1 Hydraulic pressure test: The body and lid assembly shall withstand a hydraulic test pressure
of 4 times the working pressure for 60 seconds without rupture or permanent deformation.
6.2 Safety device operation test: The primary pressure-limiting device shall open at not more
than 115 kPa and reseat at not less than 60 kPa, verified over 100 consecutive test cycles.
6.3 Drop test: The cooker (fully assembled, empty) shall be dropped from a height of 0.9 m onto
a concrete floor. No cracking, deformation of the body, or failure of safety devices shall occur.
6.4 Gasket durability: After 5 000 cycles of pressurisation to working pressure and depressurisation,
the gasket shall maintain a seal without leakage.

CLAUSE NOTES:
- Clause 5.1 (primary relief valve) and 5.3 (lid interlock) are the most critical safety clauses.
- Clause 6.1 (hydraulic test) is the mandatory factory floor test before release.
- All pressure cookers sold in India must carry the BIS mark — this is a mandatory product.
