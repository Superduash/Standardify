IS 9002:2025
SAFETY REQUIREMENTS FOR TOYS — GENERAL REQUIREMENTS
(Supersedes IS 9002:2012 [SAMPLE])

1. SCOPE
1.1 This standard lays down the safety requirements and test methods for toys intended for use
by children up to and including 14 years of age. It covers toys manufactured from or incorporating
plastic, metal, wood, textile, rubber, or combination of these materials.
1.2 The safety requirements address:
    (a) Mechanical and physical safety
    (b) Flammability
    (c) Chemical safety (migration of heavy metals and other hazardous substances)
    (d) Electrical safety (for battery-operated and mains-operated toys)
    (e) Acoustic properties (noise level)
1.3 This standard does not cover fireworks, air guns, slings, and professional sports equipment.

3. DEFINITIONS
3.1 Toy: Any product or material designed or clearly intended for use in play by children under 14 years.
3.2 Age grading: The minimum age for which a toy is considered safe, as determined by the
developmental stage and size of the child.
3.3 Small part: Any object or part of an object that, when tested as specified, fits entirely
into the small parts cylinder defined in Annex A.
3.4 Projectile: An object designed or intended to be launched or propelled so as to move through
the air or another medium.

4. MECHANICAL AND PHYSICAL REQUIREMENTS
4.1 General: Toys shall be designed and manufactured in such a way that, taking into account
the foreseeable use by children and the normal use for which the toy is intended, they do not
cause bodily injury.
4.2 Accessible edges: Edges and tips on toys intended for children under 8 years shall not be
sharp. Accessible edges shall be tested in accordance with Annex B of this standard.
4.3 Small parts: Toys intended for children under 3 years shall not contain small parts (as
defined in 3.3) when tested per Annex A. Toys for children between 3 and 6 years that contain
small parts shall carry a warning label "CHOKING HAZARD — small parts, not for children under 3."
4.4 Projectile toys: Projectiles shall not have a kinetic energy exceeding 0.08 J at the muzzle.
Rigid projectiles shall have blunt tips with a minimum contact surface area of 20 mm².
4.5 Folding/hinging mechanisms: Toys with folding or hinging mechanisms shall not create
shear, crush, or entrapment hazards for fingers or limbs.

5. FLAMMABILITY REQUIREMENTS
5.1 Materials used in toys shall not be highly flammable. Specifically:
    (a) Textile materials (hair, clothing, wigs) shall not ignite when exposed to a small flame
        of 1 second duration and, if they ignite, the flame shall not spread at a rate exceeding
        30 mm/s.
    (b) Foam or plastic materials forming the body of a soft toy shall not propagate flame
        more than 50 mm from the point of ignition.
5.2 Toys shall not be made of easily ignitable material that could cause a burn hazard to a
child who holds it near a flame.

6. CHEMICAL REQUIREMENTS
6.1 Paints, coatings, varnishes, inks, and plastics used in toys shall conform to the migration
limits for heavy metals specified in Table 3 of this standard. The limits are:
    Antimony:  60 mg/kg
    Arsenic:   25 mg/kg
    Barium:   1000 mg/kg
    Cadmium:    1.9 mg/kg
    Chromium:  60 mg/kg
    Lead:       2 mg/kg
    Mercury:   60 mg/kg
    Selenium:  500 mg/kg
6.2 Scented toys shall not contain fragrance allergens at concentrations exceeding those
specified in Schedule II of this standard.
6.3 Toys shall not contain formaldehyde at concentrations exceeding 30 mg/kg in fabrics and
1000 mg/kg in hard materials.

CLAUSE NOTES:
- Clause 4 covers mechanical safety (most common cause of toy-related injuries).
- Clause 5 covers flammability (critical for soft toys and dolls).
- Clause 6 covers chemical safety (key for paint and coating compliance).
