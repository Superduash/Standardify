IS 9999:2026
LED LIGHTING PRODUCTS FOR GENERAL PURPOSES — SAFETY AND PERFORMANCE REQUIREMENTS

1. SCOPE
1.1 This standard specifies the safety and performance requirements for light-emitting diode (LED)
light sources and LED luminaires intended for general purpose illumination in residential,
commercial, and outdoor public spaces. It applies to LED products operating at supply voltages
from 12 V dc to 240 V ac, with a rated wattage not exceeding 100 W per module.
1.2 This standard covers:
    (a) LED retrofit lamps (direct replacements for incandescent, CFL, or halogen lamps)
    (b) LED tube lights (T8, T5 form factors)
    (c) LED downlights and panel lights
    (d) LED street lights and outdoor fixtures
1.3 This standard does not apply to LED display boards, LED decorative strings, or high-power
LED systems above 100 W per module.

3. DEFINITIONS
3.1 Luminous flux: Total quantity of light emitted by a source per unit time, expressed in lumens (lm).
3.2 Luminous efficacy: Ratio of luminous flux to the power consumed, expressed in lumens per watt (lm/W).
3.3 Colour rendering index (CRI): A measure of the ability of a light source to reveal colours
of objects faithfully in comparison with a natural light source, on a scale of 0 to 100.
3.4 Correlated colour temperature (CCT): The absolute temperature (in Kelvin) of a blackbody
whose perceived colour most closely resembles that of the light source.
3.5 Luminaire: A complete lighting unit consisting of one or more LED light sources together with
the components required to distribute, filter, or transform the light.

4. SAFETY REQUIREMENTS
4.1 Dielectric strength: LED drivers and control gear shall withstand a dielectric strength test
of 1 500 V rms for 1 minute (for SELV circuits) or 2 500 V rms (for circuits connected to mains).
4.2 Creepage and clearance: Minimum creepage and clearance distances shall be maintained as
specified in Table 1, corresponding to the rated voltage and pollution degree.
4.3 Thermal management: The LED junction temperature shall not exceed 125°C under normal
operating conditions and within the rated ambient temperature range.
4.4 Touch temperature: External surfaces of luminaires accessible to the user shall not exceed
70°C for metal surfaces and 85°C for non-metal surfaces under steady-state normal operation.
4.5 Photobiological safety: LED products shall comply with the requirements of RG-1 (Exempt Group
or Risk Group 1) as specified in IS 16064 for photobiological hazard.

5. PERFORMANCE REQUIREMENTS
5.1 Minimum luminous efficacy:
    LED retrofit lamps (≤15 W): not less than 90 lm/W
    LED tube lights (T8, ≤20 W): not less than 100 lm/W
    LED street lights: not less than 110 lm/W
5.2 Colour rendering index (CRI): All LED products for indoor residential and commercial use
shall have a CRI of not less than 80.
5.3 Power factor: The power factor of LED drivers shall not be less than 0.90 for luminaires
with power above 25 W, and not less than 0.85 for luminaires 25 W and below.
5.4 Total harmonic distortion (THD): The THD of the current drawn by the LED driver shall not
exceed 20% for products above 25 W.
5.5 Rated life: LED products shall maintain at least 70% of initial luminous flux (L70) for a
period of not less than 25 000 hours under rated conditions.

6. MARKING AND LABELLING
6.1 LED lamps and luminaires shall be marked with the following information:
    (a) BIS mark and licence number
    (b) Rated wattage (W)
    (c) Rated luminous flux (lm)
    (d) Rated CCT (K) — e.g., "3000 K (Warm White)"
    (e) CRI value
    (f) Rated voltage and frequency
    (g) Manufacturer's name and country of origin
6.2 The energy label prescribed by the Bureau of Energy Efficiency (BEE) shall be affixed as
specified in the Standards and Labelling Programme for LED lamps.
