IS 9004:2025
LABELLING OF PRE-PACKAGED FOODS — GENERAL REQUIREMENTS

1. SCOPE
1.1 This standard specifies the general requirements for labelling of pre-packaged foods sold in
India. It applies to all pre-packaged foods whether domestically manufactured or imported, except
where specific product standards prescribe additional or different requirements.
1.2 This standard is applicable to foods packaged in solid, liquid, or semi-liquid form in any
container (tin, bottle, box, bag, pouch, or wrapper) that is sealed before sale to the consumer.
1.3 This standard is complementary to FSSAI labelling regulations and does not override Food
Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI) requirements.

2. REFERENCES
2.1 IS 9876:2024 [SAMPLE] — Packaged Drinking Water — Labelling Requirements
2.2 FSSAI Food Safety and Standards (Labelling and Display) Regulations, 2020
2.3 Codex Alimentarius General Standard for the Labelling of Pre-packaged Foods (CXS 1-1985)

3. DEFINITIONS
3.1 Pre-packaged food: Food that is packed and sealed prior to being offered for sale.
3.2 Ingredient: Any substance used in the manufacture of a food product that is present in the
finished product (possibly in a modified form).
3.3 Additive: A substance intentionally added to food for a technological purpose.
3.4 "Best before" date: The date after which the product may not have the expected quality,
though it may still be safe if stored correctly.
3.5 "Use by" date: The date after which consumption of the product is not recommended for
safety reasons.

4. MANDATORY LABEL DECLARATIONS
4.1 Every pre-packaged food label shall include the following declarations in a language
understood by the consumer (English and/or official regional language):
    (a) Product name/description — the common or generic name of the food
    (b) List of ingredients — in descending order of proportion by weight
    (c) Nutritional information table — per 100 g or 100 mL serving, including:
        - Energy (kcal and kJ)
        - Total fat, saturated fat, trans fat (g)
        - Total carbohydrate, sugars (g)
        - Dietary fibre (g)
        - Protein (g)
        - Sodium (mg)
    (d) Name and address of manufacturer, packer, or importer
    (e) Net content (weight, volume, or count) with legally correct units
    (f) Batch number or lot identification
    (g) Date marking: "Best Before" or "Use By" or "Expiry Date" as appropriate
    (h) Country of origin (for imported foods)
    (i) FSSAI licence number
    (j) BIS certification mark where applicable (mandatory for certain food categories)

5. ALLERGEN DECLARATIONS
5.1 Where a food contains any of the following allergens, the allergen(s) shall be declared
prominently on the label, in bold or contrasting type or font:
    - Cereals containing gluten (wheat, barley, rye, oats, and their hybrids)
    - Crustaceans and crustacean products
    - Eggs and egg products
    - Fish and fish products
    - Peanuts and peanut products
    - Soybeans and soybean products
    - Milk and milk products (including lactose)
    - Tree nuts and tree nut products
5.2 The declaration shall use the phrasing "Contains [allergen name]" in a box or highlighted
section distinct from the ingredient list.

6. NUTRIENT CONTENT AND HEALTH CLAIMS
6.1 Claims such as "low fat," "high fibre," "reduced sodium," or "source of calcium" shall only
be made if the food meets the compositional criteria specified in Schedule III of this standard.
6.2 Health claims implying a relationship between a nutrient or food component and a disease
shall not be made unless specifically authorized by FSSAI.
6.3 Front-of-pack labelling (FOPL): For products exceeding threshold levels of salt, sugar, or
total fat, an appropriate cautionary symbol (as per FSSAI FOPL regulations) shall be displayed
on the front of the package.

CLAUSE NOTES:
- Clause 4 is the primary compliance checklist for any packaged food label audit.
- Clause 5 (allergen declaration) is a new mandatory requirement added in this 2025 edition.
- Clause 6 governs marketing claims — most common source of misleading food labels.
