IS 9876:2024
PACKAGED DRINKING WATER — LABELLING AND COMPOSITION REQUIREMENTS

1. SCOPE
1.1 This standard specifies the requirements for labelling, physicochemical composition, and
microbiological quality of packaged drinking water (PDW) sold in rigid or semi-rigid plastic
containers. It applies to:
    (a) Packaged natural mineral water
    (b) Packaged drinking water from treated municipal or ground sources
1.2 This standard applies to packaged drinking water sold in containers from 200 mL to 20 L.
1.3 This standard shall be read in conjunction with IS 9001:2025 [SAMPLE] for requirements
pertaining to the plastic container itself.

2. REFERENCES
2.1 IS 9001:2025 [SAMPLE] — Drinking Water Containers Made of Plastics
2.2 IS 10500:2012 — Drinking Water Specification (for composition limits)
2.3 FSSAI Regulations on Packaged Drinking Water (Food Safety and Standards (Food Products
    Standards and Food Additives) Regulations, 2011)

3. DEFINITIONS
3.1 Packaged drinking water: Water that is sealed in a container and intended for drinking.
3.2 Mineral water: Naturally occurring underground water that has been collected at source and
contains dissolved minerals in defined concentrations.
3.3 Treated water: Water obtained from any source and subjected to filtration, purification,
or other treatment processes to meet potability standards.

4. LABELLING REQUIREMENTS
4.1 Mandatory information: All packaged drinking water containers shall bear the following
information on the label, clearly legible:
    (a) Product name: "Packaged Drinking Water" or "Packaged Natural Mineral Water" as applicable
    (b) Net content (volume in mL or L) in a font size proportional to the container size
        (minimum 3 mm for containers < 1 L, minimum 4 mm for containers ≥ 1 L)
    (c) Name, address, and contact details of the manufacturer/packer
    (d) Batch number / Lot number prefixed by "Batch No.:" or "Lot No.:"
    (e) Date of manufacture (DOM) in DD/MM/YYYY format
    (f) Best-before date in DD/MM/YYYY format — not more than 12 months from DOM for PET
        containers and 24 months for HDPE containers
    (g) Storage conditions: "Store in a cool, dry place, away from direct sunlight"
    (h) BIS certification mark and licence number
4.2 Prohibited claims: Labels shall not carry claims such as "cures disease," "miracle water,"
"alkaline" (unless pH is ≥ 8.0 and verified), or any claim that implies the water has
therapeutic properties, unless supported by FSSAI-approved clinical evidence.
4.3 Nutritional information: If any dissolved minerals are added or naturally present at
significant levels (> 10% of RDA), a nutritional information table shall be provided.

5. PHYSICOCHEMICAL COMPOSITION REQUIREMENTS
5.1 The following parameters shall comply with IS 10500:2012 limits:
    pH:                  6.5 to 8.5
    Total dissolved solids (TDS):  ≤ 500 mg/L (desirable); ≤ 2000 mg/L (permissible)
    Turbidity:           ≤ 1 NTU (desirable); ≤ 5 NTU (permissible)
    Nitrate (as NO3):    ≤ 45 mg/L
    Fluoride (as F):     1.0 mg/L (desirable); 1.5 mg/L (permissible)
    Arsenic (as As):     ≤ 0.01 mg/L
    Lead (as Pb):        ≤ 0.01 mg/L

5.2 Disinfection residuals: For treated water, the free residual chlorine at point of filling
shall be ≤ 0.2 mg/L.

6. MICROBIOLOGICAL REQUIREMENTS
6.1 Packaged drinking water shall be free from pathogenic microorganisms.
6.2 The following microbiological limits shall be met:
    Total coliform:      Not detectable in 100 mL
    Faecal coliform (E. coli): Not detectable in 100 mL
    Heterotrophic plate count (HPC): ≤ 500 CFU/mL at 35°C/48 h

CLAUSE NOTES:
- Clause 4 is the most commonly checked during inspections (labelling violations are frequent).
- Clause 5 covers water quality parameters (cross-reference IS 10500:2012).
- Clause 6 covers microbiological safety (critical for consumer health).
