IS 9001:2025
DRINKING WATER CONTAINERS MADE OF PLASTICS — SAFETY AND QUALITY REQUIREMENTS

1. SCOPE
1.1 This standard specifies the requirements for rigid plastic containers, including bottles, jars,
cans, and carboys, intended for storing, transporting, or dispensing drinking water or other
potable liquids for human consumption. It applies to containers with a capacity ranging from
200 mL to 25 L.
1.2 This standard covers containers made from the following plastic materials:
    (a) Polyethylene terephthalate (PET/PETE)
    (b) High-density polyethylene (HDPE)
    (c) Polycarbonate (PC)
    (d) Polypropylene (PP)
1.3 This standard does not apply to flexible pouches, disposable cups, or containers made of
materials other than those listed in 1.2.

2. REFERENCES
2.1 IS 9876:2024 [SAMPLE] — Packaged Drinking Water — Labelling and Composition Requirements
2.2 IS 10500:2012 — Drinking Water Specification
2.3 IS 12229:1987 — Specification for polyethylene terephthalate (PET) for food contact use

3. DEFINITIONS
3.1 Food-contact surface: Any surface of the container that may come into contact with drinking
water or potable liquids.
3.2 Migration limit: The maximum permissible quantity of a substance that may migrate from the
container material into the liquid it contains, expressed in mg/kg or mg/dm².
3.3 Overall migration: The total amount of all substances that migrate from the container into
a specified test liquid under defined conditions.

4. MATERIAL REQUIREMENTS
4.1 All plastics used in the manufacture of drinking water containers shall be of food-grade
quality and shall conform to the respective raw material specifications.
4.2 No recovered or recycled plastic material shall be used in any part of the container that
forms a food-contact surface, unless such material has been certified under a recognized
food-grade recycled plastic certification scheme.
4.3 The plastic shall not contain any colouring matter, anti-oxidants, stabilizers, or other
additives that are not listed in Schedule I of the Food Safety and Standards (Packaging)
Regulations as permitted for food-contact use.
4.4 Overall migration of substances from the container into food simulants shall not exceed
60 mg/kg or 10 mg/dm², whichever is applicable, as per IS 9833.

5. PHYSICAL AND MECHANICAL REQUIREMENTS
5.1 Containers shall be capable of withstanding a vertical compressive load of at least 3 times
the total weight of a filled container without permanent deformation exceeding 10% of original
height.
5.2 Containers shall not show leakage, cracking, or deformation when subjected to a drop test
from a height of 1.2 m onto a rigid surface, when filled with water to 90% of nominal capacity.
5.3 The wall thickness at any point shall not be less than 0.25 mm for bottles up to 2 L and
not less than 0.50 mm for containers above 2 L capacity.
5.4 The closure (cap/lid) shall form a hermetic seal. When tested at an internal pressure of
30 kPa for 30 seconds, no leakage shall occur.

6. LABELLING REQUIREMENTS
6.1 Each container shall be permanently marked or labelled with the following information:
    (a) Name and address of the manufacturer
    (b) BIS certification mark and licence number
    (c) Plastic identification code (resin code) as per IS 14534
    (d) "Food Grade" or "Safe for Food Use" declaration
    (e) Maximum temperature of use (°C)
    (f) "Do not use if damaged" advisory
6.2 The label shall be in English and may additionally be in any language listed in the Eighth
Schedule of the Constitution of India.
6.3 The plastic identification code shall be placed inside the universal recycling symbol and
shall be of a height not less than 6 mm.

CLAUSE NOTES:
- Clause 4 covers material safety (migration limits are key for compliance testing).
- Clause 5 covers physical durability.
- Clause 6 covers labelling obligations.
